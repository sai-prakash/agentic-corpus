# Method

A concept file is allowed only if **three** are true:

1. At least two independent sources in the last 14 days (paper + post, or two papers, or paper + shipping repo). A single viral thread is a *signal*, not a concept.
2. You can write the idea as a triple or a contrast (`X is not Y`).
3. Someone could ship a 6-hour lab from it.

## Intake order

1. arXiv / surveys (durable)
2. Shipping repos and product traces
3. X threads with saves/bookmarks, not just likes
4. Your own labs (first-party evidence)

## Heat

5 paper + threads + demo; 4 paper or serious repo; 3 repeated X language; 2 vocabulary; 1 ads/coins.

Every edge in graph.json must name a source id.
