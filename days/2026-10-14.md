ReHarness (arXiv:2610.12668, Xiao et al. MIT/Meta, submitted 8 Oct; X signal 13 Oct via DAIR.AI @dair_ai, 289 likes / 341 bookmarks): an agent harness for continual learning is not a forward-only reflection bank. ReHarness adds backward revision: after each instance the finished trajectory revises the specific reflections that guided it, grounding updates in source trajectories under a strictly online streaming protocol. Across three continual-learning benchmarks (16 tasks) and three backbones it outperforms raw-context, memory, reflection, and prompt-optimization baselines; revisions are source-grounded and correct overgeneralized or outdated lessons under distribution shift. Paper + independent post. Heat 4. Contrast with add/delete-only memory banks that ignore downstream outcomes.
