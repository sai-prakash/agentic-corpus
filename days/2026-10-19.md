# 2026-10-19

Harness² (Google Cloud AI Research + Northwestern; arXiv:2610.14224 submitted 9 Oct by Xu et al.; open-sourced ~8 Oct as google-research/harness2 under Apache-2.0, ~6.7k stars, GitHub hot pick 19 Oct): recursive agent harnessing for open-world tasks unseen at design time. Two levels while weights stay frozen: task-level recursion via contrastive Propose–Probe–Compose (propose edits, probe on same task, compose from behavioral contrasts, no ground-truth verifier); harnessing-level recursion updates a persistent improvement harness with adopted harnesses and evidence-supported lessons. Eight editable components (system prompt, guardrails, memory, tool guidance, subagents, skills, scripts, plugins). Supports parallel/sequential recursion, OpenCode/Codex. Gains: single step up to +11.5 on WorkBuddy-Bench, +4.5 JobBench, +11.8 pp LAB partial pass on GLM-5 / Gemini 3.5 Flash; further with higher k. Contrast: not offline Meta-Harness or GT-dependent RSI; online, verifier-free, experience-accumulating. Passes METHOD (paper + shipping repo + recent X). Heat 5. Concept written.

HERMES (arXiv:2610.07832, 6 Oct, Jin et al.): harness engineering for SE via modular executable Dev-Primitives. Each repo artifact pairs with a resident LLM for agent-native interface; dynamic activation + diagnosis-driven revision. Outperforms matched baselines by 12.4% avg across four SE benchmarks; with strong models stays near homogeneous GPT-5.6 Sol while cutting cost 26.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.0. Signal, heat 4 (paper; no independent shipping repo yet confirmed).

CorpusMap (arXiv:2609.37226, 29 Sep, Jeong et al. KAIST/Microsoft): offline entity-document bipartite map so agents follow links instead of rediscovering relations. +6.4–11.7 quality, 34–57% fewer tokens vs raw-corpus agentic search. Older than 14d window but still cited; heat 4 signal.
