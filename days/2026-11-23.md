# 2026-11-23

**MemFolder (Structure-guided folding of memory records is not free summarization or pruning)**: arXiv:2611.26427 (Xuyang Liu, Chenwen Lu, Junxi Wang et al. / Sichuan University and others, submitted 19 Nov 2026). RecordFolder uses entity-grounded anchors, semantic/entity affinity, and temporal chaining to consolidate compatible records; ContextFolder compact-encodes groups with verification feedback for recovery. On Mem0, RecordFolder retains 99.0% of original average performance across LoCoMo, PersonaMem, and LongMemEval with 50% fewer records, exceeding the strongest baseline by 1.9 points. Contrast: pruning discards evidence that later queries need; plain consolidation produces fewer but longer units that still cost attention. Heat 4. Paper + shipping GitHub repo (xuyang-liu16/MemFolder).

**KnowledgeClaw (Omnimodal typed hypergraph with closed-loop lifecycle and multi-granular routing is not partial RAG or static memory agents)**: arXiv:2611.24294 (Haoran Luo, Jiapu Wang, Linhao Luo, Luu Anh Tuan, submitted 18 Nov 2026). Formalizes OmniKE and releases OmniKE-Bench (1,829 model-free items over 3,960 real files in five modalities). KnowledgeClaw builds an omnimodal typed hypergraph, maintains it with a closed-loop lifecycle agent, and routes queries to the needed granularity. Ranks first on all six tasks against 30 baselines with overall OKE 55.9 (+14.6 over strongest). Contrast: existing systems meet at most two of omnimodality, closed-loop lifecycle, and multi-granular application. Heat 4. Paper + public software and data (LHRLAB/KnowledgeClaw).
