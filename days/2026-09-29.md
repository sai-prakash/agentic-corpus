AgentWorld (2609.31590, Shu / Zhang / Cho et al., listed this window + github.com/openagents-org/agentworld + agentworld.io): a pass rate is not collaboration. 100 human tasks + 100 variants, 50+ rounds, 3–20 asymmetric agents, no shared internals. Causal Collaboration Effectiveness traces the action graph backward from the win and scores what fraction of team effort actually caused it. Gemini 3 Flash 52.0% success / CCE 0.320; Claude Haiku 4.5 0.294; GPT-5 Mini 0.206; DeepSeek R1-70B 0.125. Failures cluster on communication breakdown, role confusion, and dropped shared plans. Heat 5 under evals-gates. Pair with Agensh: more workers and a causal share of the win are two different scoreboards. Do not confuse with the on-chain AgentWorld.me coin wrap — heat 1, skip.

Active Provenance Gate (2609.31422, Masłowski / Chudziak, listed this window): a fluent debate summary is not a sourced consensus. Multi-agent debate synthesizers invent a compromise the log does not support. APG treats the debate record as a hard constraint, audits claims, self-corrects, then blocks unsupported lines and emits a divergence report. Crisis sims: self-heal more than doubles Provenance Fidelity in the hard cells before the strict gate fires. Human study: >75% prefer an explicit-failure report in critical scenarios even when the fabricated consensus reads better. Heat 5 under evals-gates. Pair with CARGO: punishing the wrong entity and inventing a compromise that nobody said are two judges that never saw the live record.

Grow the Harness, Not the Context (2609.26760, Li / Li / Zhao / Ye / Li / Xu / Gao, v2 24 Sep, new this harvest): recurring control is not context. Growing Harness starts from a strategy-free scaffold (fixed model + tool interfaces, no task controller). Function-level traces localize failures; an optimizer repairs a window jointly; a success-first held-out gate rolls back sequences that hurt prior capability. Accepted edits accumulate in one shared harness. BrowseComp-Plus + WebArena-Verified, 4B–120B: highest mean success in 5/6 settings, −0.7 pp in the sixth. vs Tool-Calling: LLM calls −76.0–91.8%, deployed inference cost −74.4–98.6%. WebArena-Verified success stays 44.7–45.3% across scales; Tool-Calling drops to 6.7% at 4B. Ablations: traces, joint repair, and rollback each add success. No public repo this window. Heat 5 under harness-evolution / agent-harness. Pair with MoMHa: joint search over the cage file and failure-guided code growth are two ways to refuse the per-task ReAct tax.

DynBranch (2609.31047, Shen / Wadlom / Su / Lu, listed 25 Sep, new this harvest): an unresolved branch is not uncacheable. Agentic workflows serialize on branch resolution even when the downstream subgraph is predictable or already ran. DynBranch gives the unresolved branch a stable coordinate so candidate subgraphs can run during resolution and completed results reuse later. Two-level controller admits work only when expected benefit beats load price. Sits at the model-API boundary; no harness or engine change. Four agentic workloads, Qwen3-32B / 4×H200: mean latency −32% vs each workload's strongest prior, −46–66% vs no-reuse; 2.5% of generation-and-scoring time. Holds on Qwen3-8B / RTX 4090. Heat 4 under agent-harness. Serving paper — do not open a product node.

Multi-agent Scaling Across Disjunctive and Compensatory Tasks (2609.31563, Fortuna / Bertalanic, listed this window): more agents is not a scaling law. Steiner's taxonomy: plurality on disjunctive items converges to the model's modal answer; averaging on compensatory items converges to the model's item-level bias. 13 open-weight models, teams to 30. Disjunctive: P(at least one correct) +5–20 pp with team size, but plurality realizes almost none of it (within 0.5 pp of the single-model prediction). Multi-round revision helps about as much with one peer as with 29. Fermi: shared item-level bias is ~87% of squared error; averaging cuts error ~6%. Mixing families helps Fermi, does not beat the strongest member on disjunctive. Heat 4 under evals-gates. Pair with AgentWorld: headcount and causal share of the outcome are still not the same measurement.
