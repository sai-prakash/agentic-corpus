# 2026-11-24

**Harness recovery gap (public recovery features are exercised but rarely fully recover)**: arXiv:2611.27090 (Kaviru Hapuarachchi, submitted 20 Nov 2026). Graded 50 agent systems (12 persistent runtimes, 26 coding-agent harnesses, 12 research runtimes) on eight recovery mechanisms from public reports and docs. Of 128 real-use reports, only 19 describe a full recovery (54 partial, 45 failed). Checkpoint/resume recovered in 1 of 27 reports; rollback/undo in 1 of 15. Trace logs performed better (7 of 25, never outright failed). No shipping harness offers compensation for external side effects. Contrast: presence and exercise of a recovery feature is not the same as operational recovery of the run. Heat 4.
