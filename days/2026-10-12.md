E-Ledger / WorldAbduct (arXiv:2610.11552, submitted 8 Oct; official code HKUST-KnowComp/E-LEDGER-WorldAbduct): an agent harness under partial observability is not a tool dispatcher. E-Ledger adds a code-based policy approval layer before any action and a world ledger of verified hidden rules + evidence-backed dynamic state. WorldAbduct evolves it by diagnosing trajectories across four views (state consistency, world-observation gap, policy-gate correctness, goal judgment), hypothesizing latent rules, and verifying them with targeted abductive probes before ledger integration. On World of Workflows it lifts safe task completion 5–15 pp over the strongest evolution baseline across four backbones (STCR 65–80, PSR 95–100); carries to ScienceWorld (71.7% success) and DiscoveryWorld (93.8% success, ~1/3 the steps of ReAct).
