Compaction harm (2610.08722, Egor Pakhomov / Erik Nijkamp, submitted 6 Oct): a token budget is not a harm predictor. TRACE's public corpus of 590 harness-triggered AppWorld compaction boundaries replays each boundary from a re-executed prefix under the pre-compaction context and under the summary, and records the burden of the next actions (calls that error, or that repeat a call already made). Pre-boundary history predicts post-compaction harm only weakly. An internally prespecified contrast by prefix placement is a wide null; the naive "has-written" label measures trajectory phase. The best extension-protocol trigger reaches held-out AUROC 0.66 (0.64 on the replicate's own label) against a same-boundary replicate of 0.72. The best frozen, interpretable trigger avoids 21% of harmful (positive-burden) boundaries while keeping 84% of compaction opportunities, and exceeds the random-rule expectation on count but not on burden mass (a post hoc comparison). Whether that trigger beats a token-budget rule at matched retention cannot be evaluated on the release. No code URL. Heat 4 under compaction-trigger. Second source in the window is TRACE return admission (2609.33517): that paper gates what may re-enter memory; this one asks whether history can say when a summary will hurt. Do not read 0.66 as a policy.

EMHO (2610.08432, Hyun Jung Lee / Jungtaek Kim / Jongwon Jeong / Tae-Eui Kam / Donghyun Kim / Yong Jae Lee, submitted 6 Oct): a recovery prompt is not a harness revision. The embodied model stays frozen. EMHO revises the harness from execution trajectories and prior harness history under sparse environmental feedback, changing how the agent monitors progress, uses vision tools, grounds observations, and responds to failures. EMHO-Merge uses episode-level gains and losses so one shared harness can cover multiple subtasks without a silent trade-off. On EmbodiedBench navigation and manipulation it improves task success for Qwen 9B and 27B. The abstract does not print the deltas. No code URL. Heat 4 under experience-trace-harness. Second source: Causal Improvement Graph (2610.05039) externalizes the improvement state so a proposer reads relations; EMHO reads traces and rewrites the harness. Pair, do not merge.

Transect (2610.08364, Toby D. Pilditch / Konstantinos Voudouris / Alexandra Abbas / Cozmin Ududec, submitted 6 Oct): a judged label is not an observation. Long-horizon transcripts narrow the observability envelope, and an LLM assistant used to classify the transcript adds degrees of freedom that the evaluation then cannot audit. Transect, on Inspect Scout, keeps task context and behavioural vocabulary in a reusable evaluation-family config, and puts judge models in a separate setting. Reports align events, token use, sub-agent activity, and model-generated labels on one turn timeline, and each label traces to source turns. The demonstration is an AI R&D evaluation of almost 13 million tokens: operational work and manuscript production, little evidence of a sustained hypothesis-generation stage. Shipping repo: https://github.com/AI-Safety-Institute/transect. Heat 4 under observability-envelope. Paper plus shipping repo. Do not read the phase split as a score.

MINDSET (2610.08586, Sujato Dutta / Sreekruthy Tummala / Shashank Vanga / Ayushmi Pavani, submitted 6 Oct): continual summarization is not a schema transition. Episodes stay immutable. Versioned schemas change by a minimum-energy transition: reinforce, supersede, split, or create. The energy balances distortion, contradiction, historical damage, fragmentation, and internal inconsistency; hysteresis blocks an isolated contradiction from rewriting a stable schema. On a reproducible sample of 850 questions (700 LoCoMo + 150 MemoryAgentBench) it records the highest observed LoCoMo answer F1 and improves Recall@8, MRR, and nDCG@8 over LightMem (p<0.01 after Holm correction). MemoryAgentBench differences are small. Ablations name controlled fragmentation and schema-aware assignment as the largest contributors. A 700-question cross-model check used GLM-4.7 and Gemma-4-31B. The abstract does not print the F1. No code URL. Heat 4 under schema-memory. Second source: memory width vs depth (2610.08300). Width buys accuracy; depth does not. MINDSET says the unit that should change is a versioned schema, not a deeper summary. Do not read "highest observed" as a printed margin.

ParanoiaEval (2610.08662, Hanjun Luo / Xiucheng Zhang / Zhuoning Xu / Zhimu Huang / Yingbin Jin / Xinfeng Li / Hanan Salam, submitted 6 Oct): task capability is not risk-treatment. Two hundred evidence-controlled repository-level pairs differ only in the evidence that should pick Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, or Accept. On 8 models, unnecessary risk treatment occurs in 11.2%–58.7% of runs despite explicit evidence, and stronger task capability does not imply more appropriate treatment. A human-calibrated agentic judge scores violations and evidence responsiveness. No code URL. Heat 4 under evals-gates. One paper. Not a concept file.

BOTTLED (2610.08775, Ankit Sonthalia / Haritz Puerto / Alexander Rubinstein / Martin Gubri / Seong Joon Oh, submitted 6 Oct): zero-shot skill is not a bottled artifact. Agents receive an unlabelled workload and a fixed budget and may train a small model or write a program. Across ten models and three tasks, 48 of 60 bottling runs score below the lower bound of the 95% confidence interval of that model's zero-shot performance, and 31 of 60 underperform the stronger of two small-model distillation baselines at the same token budget. On query-product relevance, Opus 5 retains about 82% of its zero-shot macro-F1 at roughly 657 times lower reported cost, and about 94% of Jev's macro-F1 at a quarter of Jev's projected full-workload cost. No code URL. Heat 4 under agent-harness. One paper. Do not read 657× as a general compression rate.

VERA (2610.05923, Junqi Liu / Yongyang Pan / Zhuosong Jiang and coauthors, NVIDIA and academic labs, project lead Yucheng Tang, submitted 5 Oct; recap @AIWith_Marcus status 2107860198505283765 on 7 Oct): a model update is not a harness edit. VERA builds resumable sandboxes from trajectories: an agent writes rubrics and executable checks, a judge verifies the sandbox, and only those that pass enter the bank. Training then alternates rubric-reward model updates and harness-skill edits. A verifier gates checkpoints and harness edits on development-set criteria. Open corpus of 9,000+ long-horizon environments. A 9B model with its co-evolved agent beats the strongest baseline by 10.3 and 13.0 points in the two domains. At 27B it surpasses the baseline on AutoCoWorkBench (71.6) and AutoMedBench (80.7), transfers to unseen workflows, and retains general capabilities. The abstract does not name the baselines. The recap adds a 5-point harness gate, a 20% checkpoint reject, and a comparison to Claude Opus 4.8; those figures are not in the abstract, so they stay out of the graph. Heat 4 under gated-coevolution. Second source: VeriFine (2610.08761) co-evolves policy and judge. VERA gates model and harness; VeriFine gates policy and judge. No code URL on the abs page.
