Multi-Harness RL (2609.04518, Le / Cheng / He / Li / Li / Chen, 3 Sep, listed 7 Sep): a multi-harness recipe mixes two choices — expose the policy to several cages, and compare their rewards inside one GRPO group. They isolate the second. One Qwen3-8B SFT warm start; frozen task–harness records from Aider, OpenHands, Qwen Code, and SWE-agent; Within (one group per task–harness pair) vs Cross (harnesses pooled inside a task); sealed SWE-bench Verified oracle on the four source harnesses and a minimal harness held out of training. The evaluation harness is the dominant variable: across 24,000 sealed evaluations it moves mean solve rate from 2.14% to 9.27% (×4.3); the training recipe moves it by 1.16. On the held-out harness, Cross minus Within is +0.25 pp, 95% CI [−0.48, +1.02] at eight attempts, and +0.16 [−0.41, +0.72] pooled over three seeds that change sign. Each rule's own seed range (0.42–0.45 pp) exceeds the difference between them. An out-of-fold classifier recovers the generating harness from Cross's advantage (+4.48 pp over shuffled labels) and from Within's not at all; both rules still land the same held-out score and the same action distribution *inside* each harness. On-policy re-collection of half the data does not change this. Cross-harness credit yields configuration adaptation, not portable capability. Pair with Same Model, Different Harness and EvoHarnessBench: those change the cage at eval time; this shows RL on several cages still scores the cage.

Endogenous authorization laundering (2609.01836, Cerruti / Okamoto / Erol, 1 Sep; HF daily 4 Sep): a memory write is not a grant. Persistent stores carry permissions, restrictions, and revocations. When the store invents authority the history never issued, the agent acts on its own records with no attacker. EAL-Bench: five writer models, two executors, procurement / cybersecurity / finance. Incremental updates create false authority for up to 50.2% of unauthorized requests; once that row exists, executors act on it in 98.6% of trials. Two safeguards — stored permissions must cite a valid source event; permission changes travel through bounded event sourcing — cut laundering and also reject more legitimate actions. Pair with Memory portability: a NOTES file is already a binding across models. Pair with Context Gets Root and Deadbugz: those promote a *tool schema*; this promotes a *memory row*. The store is part of the authorization policy.

From Language Models to World-Acting Systems (2609.04894, Zhu / Cai, 4 Sep; DAIR.AI thread 7 Sep, 41 bookmarks): model competence, harness integration, and the authority a deployment grants are three objects. Evidence organized on delegated authority, temporal persistence, and environmental coupling. Action-interface expansion is documented more convincingly than completion, recovery, authorization, or independent verification. MCP and Agent2Agent improve interoperability without establishing trustworthy delegation. Multi-agent organization buys specialization plus cost and correlated failure. Persistent simulations are not agency; bounded robotics is not unattended open-world reliability. Agenda: coupled model–harness eval, capability-based permissions, durable state, cross-agent accountability. Treat as a frame, not a new method. Pair with EAL: the missing measurement they name is the one EAL-Bench actually runs.
