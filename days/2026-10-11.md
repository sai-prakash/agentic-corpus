MESH-Harness (2610.05300, submitted 4 Oct; author X 10 Oct status 2108870657492537696): a harness is not a monolithic file. MESH-Harness splits the agent harness into functional modules with role-specific interfaces, initializes pools of implementations, and uses shared representations + full-covariance LinUCB to score and select combinations under a limited evaluation budget. A proposal model revises modules from failure traces. On text, math, code, and interactive tasks it beats Meta-Harness by 2–7 points under matched budgets; iterative optimization adds 5–8 points; total cost 14.6% lower. Paper + author post. Heat 4 under mesh-harness. Do not treat the bandit as a general skill store; it is a search over module combinations.

Harness-Search (2610.05382, submitted 4 Oct; X announcement 9 Oct): long-horizon search is not one agent. Harness-Search assigns propose (Retrieval Policy), commit (Memory Operator), and audit (Summary Auditor) to separate permission-bounded roles in a Propose-Commit-Audit loop. Improves Recall 4.6–27.9 points and Final-Answer Recall 12.3–30.1 points over strongest harness baseline on evidence-retrieval benchmarks. Heat 4 under harness-search. Pair with existing multi-agent and memory-lifecycle; the separation of duties is the durable part.
