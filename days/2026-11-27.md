**Model-driven context management**: arXiv:2611.34147 (NVIDIA, submitted 25 Nov 2026). In the open-source NOOA agent harness, models receive a context API so they decide when and what to compact instead of the harness auto-triggering summarization at a threshold. On document research (OfficeQA Pro v2) at a 32K budget, GPT-6.1 nearly triples its score (21% → 56%); Kimi reaches similar or better accuracy with up to 41% fewer input tokens. Models differ sharply in how often they call the API, how aggressively they compact, and when they start; removing a timing instruction induces "context anxiety" and earlier compaction that can hurt at larger windows. Inference engines can reuse the KV cache of the unchanged suffix after an edit, cutting prefill compute by up to 15% per request with no measurable accuracy loss. Contrast: harness-triggered summarization of the oldest history is not model-controlled inspection, selective collapse, and later retrieval of originals. Heat 5.
