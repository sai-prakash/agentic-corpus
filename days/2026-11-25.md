# 2026-11-25

**SuSi-RAG subgraph similarity (production GraphRAG for maintenance causal chains)**: arXiv:2611.30588 (submitted 23 Nov 2026, Bosch + collaborators). Hybrid Graph RAG matches queries to maintenance-event subgraphs with expert-weighted entities and partial-match penalties, plus vector fallback. Deployed on an active Bosch production line for >8 months, 10,664 Q&A interactions, upper-bound answer accuracy 96.25% (confirmed error 3.75%). PoC: top-1 retrieval 61.5%→96.2%, expert-rated quality 44.9%→84.1% vs vector baseline. Contrast: subgraph similarity over structured maintenance events is not vector chunk similarity for queries with complex causal chains or cross-domain synthesis. Heat 4. Paper + independent X linking (IR papers account) + production deployment evidence.

**Independent policy distillation for modular long-term memory**: arXiv:2611.30230 (23 Nov) distills memory-writing and sequential retrieval policies independently from frontier trajectories into 3B models, then composes them with a fixed answer generator (no joint training). Achieves 73.1% on LongMemEval-based benchmark vs 36.2% for composition of existing RL specialists; further gains with light adaptation. Related within 14 days: arXiv:2611.26361 (19 Nov) on self-distilled lessons/guidelines for agent memory with model-dependent optimal size. Contrast: composing independently distilled specialist policies is not the same as joint optimization or distilling everything into one set of weights. Heat 4.
