# 2026-11-26

**Recursive Collective Intelligence (RCI) on an equal-budget ladder**: arXiv:2611.32606 (submitted 24 Nov 2026, Stanford + collaborators). Grades multi-agent groups against compute-matched independent agents at three levels (single agent, majority vote, pass@n). Interaction improves the group only when it moves information agents cannot gather alone (fact-forwarding lifts above pass@n at 9B/27B/35B scales). Organization emerges spontaneously among identical agents; encoding it in a prompt reaches level 3 at half the tokens. Groups recursively improve themselves label-free: successive generations climb the ladder and outperform predecessors on unseen problems for 4–6 generations on PlanBench, HiddenBench, and Hanabi. Contrast: a team that merely votes or shares context is not one that moves scarce information and then trains its next generation on its own verified turns at equal cost. Heat 4.
