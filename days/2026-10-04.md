ActiveSaddler (2610.00906, Park / Kim / Zhang / Han / Gao / Park / Yao / Fu / Nallipogu / Lin / Rühle, submitted 1 Oct, announced 2 Oct; POSTECH / KAIST / Microsoft + https://autosaddler-projectpage.github.io/activesaddler/): a fixed scenario order is not a curriculum. Automated harness optimizers already rewrite prompts, tool interfaces, and control logic from execution feedback. They still freeze which scenarios generate that feedback. ActiveSaddler treats the curriculum as a non-stationary bandit: recurring failures become reusable arms, each arm is scored for further learning progress, and the optimizer balances known weaknesses against unseen scenarios. On GAIA2 and Terminal-Bench 2.0, test Pass@1 rises 4.4 and 7.5 percentage points over the same harness optimizer with a scenario order fixed before optimization. The project page puts the absolutes at 59.8% vs 55.4% on GAIA2 and 80.0% vs 72.5% on Terminal-Bench 2.0, and reports the path to 58.5% dev as $298 vs $1,360. Ablations say the gain needs dynamic targets, evolving utility estimates, and a discovery balance — not a better patch writer alone. Recap on X: @Shadowfetch status 2106554619891470650. Heat 5 under harness-evolution. Pair with Growing Harness (2609.26760): that loop localizes a failure and grows a function; this loop decides which failure is worth growing next. Do not read the 7.5 point line as a new base agent.

Keyword Harnesses Fail Open (2610.02142, Juan S. Santillana, submitted 1 Oct): a keyword score is not a tool call. A matched pair of Spanish security models, 661.6M (no dedicated tool SFT) and 1,109M (web-heavy curriculum, 6B-token tool-SFT), score almost the same on a lenient tool-use metric (B4 0.660 vs 0.650). Verbatim reproduction on training examples splits them: the 600M emits valid calls with generalized arguments on 6/6; the 1B does so on 0/6 across checkpoints. A first-token probe puts the 1B's missing prior at 10^-4 to 10^-5 on the tool-call token, erased by the web-heavy phase. A targeted SFT (diverse corpus, 5x learning rate, 2,202 steps, about 3.3 GPU-hours) lifts valid emission on 269 corpus rows from 0.100 to 0.959 (600M: 0.926). On 238 unseen prompts the repaired 1B passes 0.536 vs 0.428 (p = 0.004). The repair does not move the trigger token's tied embedding (97.7% of the bf16 table stays bit-identical). Both still over-trigger on negative prompts (0.09 and 0.17). No code URL on the abs page. Heat 5 under evals-gates. Pair with Finding the Right Fit (2610.00917): one says the ranking is the pairing; the other says a lenient harness can credit a call that never happened. The ladder is minutes of CPU. Use it before a tool-use claim on a small model.

Mem++ (2610.02002, Yehia / Abdelkareem / Ahmed / Omran / Alashmouny / Claudel / Mohamed, submitted 1 Oct + github.com/AIDAChip-Inc/mem-plus-plus): write-time distillation is not a time-indexed archive. Organizational decisions arrive as new documents, not edits. Compressing each document into facts, notes, or edges fixes what can be answered before the question exists. Mem++ stores every document whole with date and author, calls no generative model at write time, and at read time retrieves only documents dated up to the time the question asks about, fusing lexical and semantic ranks. Older versions stay. On OrgMemBench it beats the strongest memory-system baseline by 8.0 to 13.1 points across two answering models. With gpt-4.1-mini it is best overall, 2.6 points above RAG. Best average LLM-judge score on LoCoMo; second on LongMemEval-S, behind only its own entity-graph variant. Heat 4 under memory-lifecycle / read-time memory. Pair with Causal Memory Policy (2610.02070): one refuses to score a memory the retriever never showed; the other refuses to throw the source document away before the question names a time.

Harness Annealing (2610.01235, Yang / Chai / Wen, submitted 1 Oct): training on the harness is not internalizing the harness. Harnesses decide what to investigate, whether to revise, and when to stop. Training on successful harness-supported trajectories can raise the score while leaving those decisions at runtime. HAT adds explicit control supervision and a curriculum of teacher trajectories collected under progressively weaker harnesses. 9B and 35B models on SWE-QA and SWE-QA-Pro, every checkpoint scored under four deployment harnesses. Selected annealed checkpoints with tools alone score close to their starting checkpoints under the full harness. Further annealing does not uniformly help, and the benefit depends on scale and deployment cage. No code URL, no point deltas in the abstract — do not invent them. Heat 4 under harness-evolution. Pair with ActiveSaddler: one anneals control out of the cage and into the weights; the other keeps the weights fixed and changes which scenarios teach the cage.

FloWright (2610.01026, Guo / Wang / Chen / Chen / Wang / Wang, submitted 1 Oct + https://xhguo7.github.io/FloWright/): optimizing the workflow generator is not co-evolving the agents that execute it. A workflow outcome is one sparse score, so extending training past the generator has no agent-level credit. FloWright uses the workflow itself as the harness: a hierarchical, structure-aware reward lets one role self-evolve and two or more roles co-evolve, with no extra models, labels, or executions. DataWright hardens existing sets into workflow-level tasks. Across document, slide, chart, code, math, and finance tasks, small open models gain up to +7.41%; co-evolving more roles is +5.03% against +2.83% for optimizing one role alone. Heat 4 under agent-harness. Pair with ActiveSaddler: one chooses the next scenario for a harness optimizer; the other credits roles inside the workflow the harness is. Do not read +7.41% as a single-benchmark SOTA.
