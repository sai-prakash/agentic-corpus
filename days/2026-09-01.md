EvoUndo (2608.28363): a capability-improving harness mutation is not an admissible mutation. Across 600 unseen one-shot self-evolution tasks, 197 mutations raise the score and then fail recoverability on counterfactual harness states. Conventional repair recovers 0/197 under the original recovery language. Exact state-address grounding lifts 0/48 → 38/48 when that language is enough; extending the recovery calculus recovers 142/143 of the remaining stratum. Snapshot-restore is not a substitute — a full snapshot clobbers later edits (0/300 selective recovery). Nash / Second Mind: if a fresh session cannot resume from an external recovery state plus the repo, the harness stored history, not operational state.

Logos (2608.28553): the composability calculus does not bind an agent to one process. Plugin = process. The only shared state is an append-only transcript. Eighty sessions resume with no repeated effect after kills at the four boundaries of the tool-call cycle. One fault in a single-process harness interrupts every co-resident session; under peer processes it ends at one node.

IBM VLDB workshop (31 Aug): how much structure should agentic graph memory materialize before retrieval? Heavy event/schema-rich construction spends the token budget on abstractions the downstream stack does not use; graph-free baselines lose multi-hop composition. In their normalized setting, an entity-centric construction with prompt/model optimization beats the high-structure baseline on answer quality and cuts construction cost by more than 10×. Recipe, not a new KG algorithm: freeze retriever and reader when you test construction; every added layer must move the quality–cost frontier.
