Neutral prompt 10.17%; CrewAI shipped prompt 24.67%. Nine production frameworks audited; none specifies the post-failure act. One sentence requiring `retrieval_status: OK|FAILED` before the answer drops dishonesty 14.10% → 0.87% (92 improve / 1 worsen of 688) and transfers into three foreign scaffolds; the flag is faithful 99.7–99.9% and regex-detectable.
