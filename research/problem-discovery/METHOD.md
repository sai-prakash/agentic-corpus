# Problem Discovery Research Method

Purpose: identify recurring, expensive problems with real buyer urgency, measurable economic value, and a tractable solution wedge. This corpus is designed to preserve evidence and contradictions, not to justify a preselected idea.

## Research question

Where is there a recurring, expensive workflow for a clearly identifiable buyer, with demonstrated spending, poor current satisfaction, reachable data/workflows, and enough remaining solution gap for a small team to enter?

## Evidence unit

Every evidence record should capture:

- evidence_id
- date_observed / source_date
- industry
- problem_id
- persona / buyer
- source_type
- source_url
- source_quality (A-E)
- claim_or_observation
- frequency signal
- economic signal
- workaround / current tool
- dissatisfaction / failure mode
- purchase or budget signal
- contradiction / negative evidence
- geography
- notes

## Source quality

- A: primary/official dataset, regulator, audited filing, published vendor pricing
- B: strong independent research with methodology disclosed
- C: behavioral evidence: practitioner discussion, review, job posting, workflow description
- D: vendor claim or sponsored study
- E: inference derived from other evidence

Vendor claims stay vendor claims even if repeated by many vendors.

## Candidate dimensions

Do not compress everything into a single score. Track separately:

1. Pain severity
2. Recurrence / volume
3. Direct financial consequence
4. Existing spend
5. Dissatisfaction with current solution
6. Buyer clarity
7. Data accessibility
8. Integration burden
9. Regulatory / liability burden
10. Competition density
11. AI / automation fit
12. Human-review compatibility
13. Time-to-value
14. Ability to run a concierge MVP
15. Ability to validate willingness to pay quickly

## Falsification rule

Every candidate must include a reason not to build it and a cheap test that could kill the idea.

## Validation ladder

Desk research -> workflow interviews -> real artifact/data sample -> concierge service -> paid pilot -> software.

A landing-page signup is not purchase validation.

## Current corpus scope

Initial pass: healthcare administration, construction, property management, accounting/finance operations, commercial insurance, logistics/freight, cybersecurity/GRC, and quality/document-control workflows.
