# Evals & gates

**Definition.** Independent checks that keep or reject a harness change, a tool call, or a session outcome. Not a paragraph in the system prompt.

**Contrast.** Name-match tool evals are not scenario evals. Agent Seer synthesizes graded multi-turn dialogues from an MCP spec alone; the failure that matters is argument values. Harness-Bench scores the *pairing* (model + harness), not the weights. Same Model, Different Harness makes that pairing the experimental unit: tight-window policy moves F2PF 28 → 49 with frozen weights. EvoHarnessBench (2609.04280): the gate on a catalog change is backward transfer on *already solved* tasks, not only the new pass rate. Deployment and adaptation can disagree. Memory portability (2609.05339): a store-only repair of NOTES misses 90% recovery in 48/48; keep the raw history or you cannot gate a model swap. PlanFence (2609.03340): freshness of shared state is not a gate on the pending action. MASkills: a skill edit that fails held-out validation rolls back. ClaimReceipt (2609.01992): a missing receipt is INCONCLUSIVE, not a pass. SWE-Gate (2609.04167): of 644 functionally passing repairs, 221 fail review constraints. A terminal pass/fail is not a credit assignment. Interchangeability (2609.05279): swapping a role-matched agent barely moves task score and still raises communication per unit of progress 16–63%. A placebo roster shuffle is the control; greedy decoding shrinks both drift and the swap tax; longer formation history grows both. Outcome fungibility is not a coordination gate. Multi-Harness RL (2609.04518): pooling several coding harnesses inside one GRPO group is not a portability gate. Across 24,000 sealed SWE-bench Verified evals the *evaluation harness* moves mean solve rate 2.14% → 9.27% (×4.3); the training recipe moves it by 1.16. Cross minus Within on a held-out minimal harness is +0.25 pp, 95% CI [−0.48, +1.02], and seed ranges exceed that difference. Cross's advantage still names the generating harness (+4.48 pp over shuffled labels); Within's does not; both rules match held-out score and in-harness action mix. State the grouping boundary; test under an unseen cage. World-acting review (2609.04894): action-interface expansion is documented more than completion, recovery, authorization, or independent verification. EAL-Bench (2609.01836): a memory row that mints authority is a failed authorization gate — writers invent false grants on up to 50.2% of unauthorized requests; executors fire 98.6% of those rows. Verification Tax (2609.04681): coding-activity lift is not production-qualified change. The gate is reviewer-hours and residual risk, not tokens generated. Audit Without Verification (2609.07680 + Polpii/FaultLine): an auditor that reads peer conclusions is not a check. 345,600 requests per chain model. Zero allegations in 7,996 clean silent episodes. False-alarm clean runs name an innocent party 34.4% / 62.6% by chain. Origin recovery from reports 4.1% vs 60.3% from raw documentation. Deleting conclusions +41.2 pp accuracy (95% CI +35.3 to +46.9) and adherence 94.4% → 3.4%. Discovery Certification Protocol (2609.09219): a score is not a discovery. Two controlled audits, 0/96 recoveries, upper bound 0.0468; truthful feedback 30 recoveries vs 0 on a specified neutral policy. A deterministic LLM-free verifier reproduces the decision from frozen evidence. Cross-substrate authority (2609.08472): planning on workspace-visible evidence is not a publication gate — 12/16 unsafe publishes; the mutation-boundary guard is. Double Measurement Confound (2609.09218): a score is not a model measurement when the scaffold owns execution-critical decisions and the scorer grades shape. Moving those decisions onto the model and replacing shape-scoring with seeded ground truth turns a flat ComtradeBench leaderboard into a reliability spectrum; scorer validity is benchmark-specific, scaffold ownership was uncontrolled wherever they probed. CapScope (2609.08371): injected-effect rate is the authorization gate, not repair pass rate — 3/75 vs 33–47/75 with repair still 68/75. Scanning the Harness (2609.07360): install-time pin and pre-approval checks are gates on the *catalog*, not on the model. RobustSGPO (2609.09646): permission to widen the edit scope is itself a gated search policy — periodic 1→2→3 beats always-max; held-out completion 60% → 80% under a 20M-token budget. Harbor-Index v3 (2609.04298): an 80-bench adapter farm is not a score you can rerun. 82 audited hard tasks from 29 benches; no evaluated model-harness pair exceeds 30% pass; GPT-5.5 + Codex 28.0%. Ecdysis (2609.11677): a one-off fail is not a mutation ticket — recurring cross-task patterns are. ChurnBench (2609.11515 + vsingh45/churnbench): cache age is not the freshness gate. With scheduled refresh, freshness errors 7 / 4 / 4 at 1 / 14 / 28 days; disable tiered refresh and 28-day freshness errors jump 4 → 45. Gold answers come from an append-only ledger, not the live store. VP-CONTROL (2609.10969): a second model on the same evidence is not an independent commit gate — shared-evidence cross-model vote approves 62.9% of unsafe proposals vs 22.9% with an independent source; after-check races lose to a full atomic guard (0 unsafe / 216 live episodes). BenchShield (2609.11028): a score is not proof the run stayed inside the evaluation boundary — infrastructure-side lifecycle evidence lifts full-chain recall to 77–100% and runtime hack detection to 96%. Silent failures (2609.10548): 170 confirmed test-passing insecure repairs across seven frameworks; omission 48.2%. MCP registry draw (2609.10962 + itguruhaseeb/mcp-probe): a curated MCP frame is not the registry — handshake 48.8% vs 66.7% curated; BFCL raw rows are 68.8% exact repeats. pawbreak (0xgetz/pawbreak, 14 Sep): a single-prompt injection bench is not an agent red-team. Plant the payload in the file, page, mail, or tool output the hands will read; score tripwire ASR (breached ÷ valid), critical-only, and per-surface. Osmani / Cherny: the gate on production agent code is reject-before-PR plus a skill written from the miss, not a later human tidy. When Agents Disagree (2609.11709): a majority of forward traces is not an independent check — a reverse posterior plus Jensen-Shannon cross-path ranking is the label-free anchor; LogLin helps most where the agents conflict. Pair with VP-CONTROL: two forward voters on one file are still one factorization. Harness or Model? (2609.11987): an average native-vs-generic delta inside a 10 pp CI is not a ranking — publish the repo/contest split. Skill Issue (2609.12742): with-vs-without on reverted PRs is the skill gate; a +4.9 pp GEPA lift that cannot beat run variance is a signal, not a ship ticket. GuardrailLoop (2609.12216): hash-pin goal, budget, eval identity, and release bar before the loop may evolve; outcome recovery after a crash is not exactly-once (30/240 planner repeats).

Publish angle: do not admit a harness or skill edit that adds a tool and silently fails yesterday's ticket, that cannot abstain when a receipt is missing, or that fires a plan whose cited records have moved. Do not let the harness grade itself. Do not treat a role-matched swap as free because the scoreboard did not move. Do not treat Cross-harness GRPO as portable capability until an unseen cage agrees. Do not treat a memory write as a grant. Do not treat an auditor that quotes its peers as a check. Do not treat a sealed-eval score as a discovery until recovery is bounded. Do not treat a leaderboard as a model ranking until you know who owned the execution-critical step. Do not treat a 28% Harbor-Index cell as a model ranking until the pairing is named. Do not treat cache age as freshness. Do not treat a second model on the same file as a second source. Do not treat a passing unit test as a security repair. Do not treat a single-prompt scan as an agent red-team. Do not treat a quiet human fix as a passing gate. Do not treat a native-harness average as a model ranking. Do not treat a recovered outcome as an unrepeated trace. Do not treat a skill optimizer score as significant at one repository's PR count.
