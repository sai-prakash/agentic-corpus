# Evals & gates

**Definition.** Independent checks that keep or reject a harness change, a tool call, or a session outcome. Not a paragraph in the system prompt.

**Contrast.** Name-match tool evals are not scenario evals. Harness-value (2609.20474): the gate on erroneous acceptance is a standalone verifier, not the plan that raised success — 61% of invalid Retail episodes rejected, 17% of correct ones withheld, <¢t1. Coding-harness study (2609.20804): overflow-prevention is the context gate; recoverable elision is machinery without a measured accuracy gate. TRACE (2609.19897): source-traceable hit rate is the archive gate, not a new graph build. Chronicle (2609.20625 + theagentplane/chronicle): a recorded incident is not a CI test until one boundary is live. Cut-point replay serves chosen envelopes from the record and runs the complementary subset against new code. Six incidents: +23 μs/crossing to record; full replay zero model calls and bit-stable ×20; stub-every-boundary with the same assertion catches none of the guard mutants, cut-point catches all. SkillAA Local/Big Gates are the same shape on a skill graph. Verification-status laundering (2609.20211): a monitor that never sees "unverified" is not a gate. DENSE (2609.21423): a raw trace is not a gate until local progress, recovery, and unfinished obligations sit in a shortcut tree. REFIT holds the initial trajectories fixed under outcome blindness; Terminal-Bench 2.1 strict pass +7.12–15.64 pp vs those initials, 19.0–43.6% fewer recipient tokens, highest among tested non-privileged feedback methods on four models. Efficient Benchmarking in Production (2609.21267): a full suite is not the recurring gate. 574 historical runs; 2PL adaptive testing at 200 questions (38.5% of a full run) is 1.03 pp MAE; shipped difficulty-stratified fixed subsets transfer to five other agent families without recalibration. LangChain × Jev (19–20 Sep, blog.langchain.com/jev-as-a-judge-for-agent-evals + danielgshea/jev-as-a-judge): an LLM judge is not a typed decision. Five fixed Deep Agents traces × 100: Jev 100% binary oracle agreement vs Terra 99.8 / Luna 96.4 / Sonnet 4.6 80.0; quality variance 92–913× lower; $0.00035 and 0.44 s vs Claude $0.02811. Narrow corpus — heat 3 product measurement. OpenMAS-GCom (2609.21527): a G-MAS final score is not an attribution. Hold task, model, prompt, and budget; rewire edges, drop specialist or critic, poison intermediates, disable workers. 17 configs / 29 datasets / 400 G-MAS-Complex tasks. Specialist removal hurts more than critic removal; similar original scores hide different degradation. RecreationBench (2609.22000 + QwenLM/RecreationWorld): 58.1% overall with 2.8% pass-all programmatic is not a solved hybrid CUA. Pair with ContrAgent / CPV / CHASE / CapScope / VP-CONTROL / Fabrication After Tool Failure: a lift on the happy path is not a release gate until withheld-correct, Sham, a live cut-point, an aligned cheap slice, and a one-factor intervention are on the page. Agensh (2609.26781): a 1→N pass-rate lift is not a gate until wall-clock, kill budget, and merge cost sit next to it — 19.31% → 28.78% mean test-pass at 128 workers is still a ProgramBench score. Execution provenance (2609.25913 + xiaoqi-7/GraphMemory): Full Support@2048 on gold spans is the memory gate, not Hit@k on a fragment. +19.07 vs flat 512-token windows; graph propagation +4.55 only when evidence is multi-event. Approval-to-Action Security Benchmark (2609.28586): residual effects after an approved invocation are the gate, not the yes/no on the entry command — 40 → 13 residuals as the record grows semantics; binding a predicted closure cuts holdout residuals 10 → 3. Scope-matched ORC (2609.29144): a trigger-family lift that drops another family is a failed gate (6/12 harmful global accepts). Vinci Rufus (24 Sep) restates RRSI: an eval the harness was tuned on is a vanity metric. Trace tampering (2609.30266): a monitor that reads a log the agent can delete is not a gate.

Publish angle: do not treat a plan lift as a release gate until the Sham control and the withheld-correct rate are both on the page. Do not treat a stubbed replay as a regression test until one unsafe boundary runs live. Do not treat a cheap judge as a gate until variance *and* oracle agreement are both on the page. Do not treat a multi-agent scoreboard as a communication result until one factor moved. Do not treat N workers as a result until the same task is scored at N=1 with a kill budget. Do not treat a memory hit as support until the gold span set fits the budget. Do not treat an approved command as covered until the post-run effect set matches a frozen prediction. Do not treat a skill accept as global until a second family is replayed.
