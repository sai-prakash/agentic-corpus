# Compositional policy

**Definition.** A policy that is a property of the whole execution trace — authority limits, referral thresholds, cumulative caps, required review — evaluated by recomputing the guarded quantity from raw provenance, not by trusting any single step's derived field.

**Contrast.** Step-level compliance is not workflow compliance. Compositional Policy Violations (2609.18820): every individual step can pass its classifier, per-turn rail, or span evaluator while the composed run violates the policy the organization actually holds. Four types, each named by the mutation site: Authority Creep (permissions accumulate and reroute later steps), Threshold Laundering (an early check is not repeated after the quantity moves), Cumulative Sum Violation (legal atoms exceed an aggregate cap — three $90 buys against a $250 daily limit), Context Collapse (faithful summaries drift; context-setting facts 25% → 9%). A predicate over one step cannot see a property that step does not determine, so better step monitors do not close the class. ContrAgent (2609.18128): the missing artifact is one deterministic contract that both gates the next tool call and scores the finished trace. Assume-guarantee contracts in LTLf compile to a DFA; the contract library is independent of the model. Matches LLM-judge and rule-based guardrail baselines on four benches with reproducible verdicts and much lower online per-call latency. Pair with runtime-consequence-control: OATS / Scan the Skill stop one prohibited *class*; this stops a *legal* class that only becomes illegal in composition. Pair with evals-gates / VP-CONTROL: two models voting on one file are still one factorization; the DFA is a second source only if it reads raw events. Pair with authorization-laundering: EAL invents a grant inside the store; CPV spends a real grant past its cap. Verification-status laundering (2609.20211) is Context Collapse at the monitor: the handoff keeps "authorized" and drops "never verified." Same risky action, provenance framing removed, approval 5% → 60% / 9% → 98% on two open-weight monitors; pipeline 57–81%. A step monitor that never sees the unverified bit cannot enforce the session policy. Safe Skill Retirement (2609.29543): a step that still passes the authorized task is not the session policy. Task-certified clause cuts drop >94% of text and keep authorized completion, then fire unauthorized protected effects in every one of 12 bundles. The second gate is a matched authority counterfactual, not another happy-path score. Skilder (2609.28693) packages the bound; retirement is what happens when maintainers delete the clause that encoded it. SpecHarness (2609.29921) is the same factorization one layer up: the actor may not sign the spec it was given.

Compositional Safety Failures in Harness Evolution (2609.33123): an individually safe *component update* is not a safe composition. Memory, prompt, skill, and tool edits that each keep utility and pass a component safety check can interact into an unsafe run. Three safety-related benches: 43 pairwise and 18 irreducible 3-way failures. Typed hypergraph of component states and higher-order safety interactions; on a change, update only the interaction neighborhood instead of reconstructing the global product. Pair with CPV: a legal atom that blows a cap and two safe diffs that compose into an unauthorized write are the same shape on different objects (trace vs cage file).
