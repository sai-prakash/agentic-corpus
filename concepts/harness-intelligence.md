# Harness intelligence

**Definition.** A trainable, transferable ability to synthesize, repair, and archive task-specific harnesses. Orthogonal to model scaling.

**Contrast.** Harness evolution is an outer loop over code. Harness intelligence is a model whose output *is* the harness. JIT-Agent freezes the generator and lets the archive compound. Four modules only: memory, planning, action, capability.

**Heat.** 5. Updated 2026-08-28.

Sources: JIT-Agent 2608.25593; github.com/bingreeky/JIT; StarHarness 2608.24804 as the AOT baseline.
